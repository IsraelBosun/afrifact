Each fact below needs a photograph. Your job is to write the search terms
someone would type into Google Images to find a picture of what the fact
is ABOUT — not a picture that illustrates the idea of it.

Rules:

1. Name the most photographable thing in the fact. A person, a building, a
   place, an object, a dish, a team. If the fact is about an event, name
   the person or the place at the centre of it.

2. Three to six words. No quotation marks, no punctuation, no "photo of",
   no "image".

3. Add the country or city only when the name alone would be ambiguous.
   "Burna Boy" needs nothing. "Sakponba Road" is better as "Sakponba Road
   Benin City".

4. Prefer the proper noun. A query of common nouns returns stock
   photography, which is the one thing that is never right here.

5. If a fact genuinely has no photographable subject, still give your best
   query — the most concrete noun in it. Something is returned for every
   fact.
