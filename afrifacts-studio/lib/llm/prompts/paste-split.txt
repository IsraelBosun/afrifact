Someone pasted a pile of facts they saw online, to be checked and added
to AfriFacts, an app of surprising, verified facts about Africa. Your job
is only to separate them: find every distinct fact in the text.

## How to separate

- A numbered or bulleted list: each item is one fact, unless one item
  plainly makes two unrelated claims, in which case it is two.
- Running prose: each separate claim is one fact. Setup and the claim it
  sets up are ONE fact, not two.
- The same fact said twice (reposted, reworded): list it once.
- Leave out what is not a fact: headings, "did you know", hashtags,
  emojis, usernames, "follow for more", opinions, jokes, questions,
  advertising.
- Keep each claim FAITHFUL to what was pasted. Tidy the wording into one
  plain sentence, but do not correct it, soften it or add to it. It will
  be checked against real sources, and a wrong claim is expected: that is
  what the checking is for.

## For each fact

- "claim": the fact as one plain sentence.
- "country": the ISO code of the African country it is about, from this
  list: {{countries}}. If it is not clearly about one of them, use
  {{fallback}}.
- "look": one to three English Wikipedia article titles most likely to
  state this fact: the person, company, event or place it is about, as
  the article would be titled ("Hushpuppi", "Kwame Nkrumah", "Dangote
  Group"). Most specific first.
