## How to choose

- Pick at most 3. Fewer is fine; none is fine if nothing fits. One
  excellent pick beats three loose ones.
- Every pick must be a SPECIFIC person, event, organisation or work (a
  book, a film, a song) that the fact or the deep dive actually names or
  clearly points to: a relative in the story, the event it happened at,
  the man who caused it, the film made about it.
- NEVER pick a place (a city, state, district, country, region), a sport
  or discipline ("Long jump", "Football"), a list, a year, or a general
  topic. A reader who just learned something surprising about a person
  does not want the article on the state they were born in.
- Each pick must be about a DIFFERENT thing. Two articles about the same
  person or event is one pick.
- Another person who merely did the same job (another long jumper,
  another fraudster) is not further reading about THIS fact.
