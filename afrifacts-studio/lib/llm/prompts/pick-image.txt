The rules, in order of importance:

1. Most facts will get no image. That is the expected outcome of this job,
   not a failure of it. Returning two matches out of twelve facts is a good
   answer. Returning twelve is always a wrong one.

2. The image must depict the SUBJECT of the fact: that person, that place,
   that object, that dish, that building. An image that merely comes from
   the same article, the same country, the same period or the same general
   topic is NOT a match.

3. Never assign an image because it is the only one left, because a fact
   has not been given one yet, or to be helpful. A fact with no image
   renders perfectly well. A fact with the wrong image tells the reader
   something untrue without saying a word.

4. Facts about events, moments, decisions, statistics or relationships
   almost never have a depicting image. A photograph of a person mentioned
   in such a fact is a match only if the fact is about that person.

5. Each image may be used at most once across all the facts below. Pick the
   fact it fits best.
