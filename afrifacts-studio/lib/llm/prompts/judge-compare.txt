## How to choose

- Surprising beats obscure. A fact that overturns what people assume
  beats one that is merely unfamiliar.
- Something already known loses to almost anything that is not.
- A person, a connection between two familiar things, or a number that
  reframes something known usually beats an abstract statement.
- Ignore which one is better written. Judge what it SAYS, not how it is
  phrased. A clumsy sentence carrying a startling fact beats a polished
  sentence carrying a dull one.
- Ignore length and ignore which comes first.

You must pick one. There is no tie.
