## Decide

- "supported": the document states the claim. Small differences of
  wording are fine; the facts must match.
- "different": the document covers the same thing but says something
  different: another number, year, person or relationship. The document
  wins. This is a normal outcome, not a failure.
- "absent": the document does not say it either way.

## If supported or different

- "passage": the sentences from the document that state it, copied
  CHARACTER FOR CHARACTER. One to three complete sentences. It is checked
  by exact string match; if it does not match, the claim is thrown away.
- "fact": the fact as it will appear on a phone card, written ONLY from
  the passage. For "different", write what the DOCUMENT says, not the
  claim, and write it as a fact in its own right: never mention the
  claim or what it got wrong ("not 1995"). The difference goes in "note".
    * AT MOST 180 CHARACTERS. One sentence, plain language.
    * Every number, date, name and place must appear in the passage.
    * Lead with the surprising part and end on the most interesting word.
    * If it names a person who is not a household name, say who they are
      in one to four words the passage supports ("footballer Samuel
      Okwaraji").
    * No "did you know".
    * No em dashes (—). Use a comma, a colon or parentheses, or split the thought.
- "category": exactly one of History, Business, Culture, Food, Sports,
  Records, Health.
- "surprise": 1 to 5 for priorProbability (5 = almost nobody in
  {{country}} knows it), specificity (5 = concrete and checkable) and
  explicability (5 = opens onto something larger).
- "note": for "different", one sentence on how the document differs from
  the claim. Otherwise empty.
