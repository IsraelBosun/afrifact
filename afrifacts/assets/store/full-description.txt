You know Nigeria. You do not know these.

AfriFacts is a feed of surprising, verified facts about Nigeria. Open it and you are already reading one. Swipe up for the next. That is the whole app.

No signup. No onboarding carousel. No wall in front of the first fact.


WHAT IS IN IT

• A full-screen feed of facts, swiped like a social app rather than scrolled like a reference list.

• Deep dives. Tap the sparkle on any fact for the longer story, ending in a "Why it matters" that connects it to something bigger than itself.

• Quick quizzes. Three questions, under thirty seconds, dropped into the feed as you read. Right or wrong, you get the fact behind the answer.

• Share cards. Any fact exports as a clean image sized for WhatsApp status, Instagram and X. Photo credits travel with it.

• Save anything. Bookmarked facts wait for you in Saved.

• Two facts a day, if you want them. One in the morning, one in the evening. Off unless you say yes, and you are asked once, politely.

• Works offline. After the first load the facts live on your phone, so a bad signal costs you nothing.

• Dark mode from the first launch, not an afterthought.


EVERY FACT CARRIES ITS SOURCE

Most facts apps invent things. This one cannot afford to.

Every fact here was pulled out of a real document and keeps the passage it came from. Nothing is written from a model's memory. Every source carries a stable reference rather than a bare link that will rot in a year. A person reviews every fact before it ships, and anything an educated Nigerian would already know is rejected before it ever reaches you.

Tap the source on any fact and go and read it yourself. That is the point of it.


NIGERIA FIRST

Nigeria is where AfriFacts starts. More of the continent follows, and the app was built so that adding a country is a matter of content rather than a new app.


Facts worth knowing, about a place worth knowing better.
